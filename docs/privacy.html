Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 | Last Updated: November 7, 2026

1. Privacy Principles & Zero Network Transmission

Stashfill is strictly offline, no-login, and privacy-first. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data. Stashfill does not maintain any backend servers, databases, or user accounts. There are zero tracking pixels, analytics probes, or telemetry tools in the extension.

2. What Information Stashfill Stores

All information you save in Stashfill—including names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, custom text snippets, and payment cards—is stored exclusively on your local device via the browser's local storage engine (browser.storage.local).

Payment Cards & Sensitive Fields: Payment cards are stored only inside an encrypted vault envelope using AES-GCM 256-bit encryption with PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 iterations). Your master passphrase is never stored on disk.

3. Permissions Requested & Technical Justification

Permission Technical Purpose
storage Store user profiles, snippets, and the encrypted vault locally on the user's computer.
activeTab Access the current webpage only when the user explicitly triggers autofill or quick-paste.
scripting Programmatically execute the form filling script in the active webpage on user request.
contextMenus Provide right-click shortcuts to fill forms or insert snippets directly into input fields.
Optional Host Permissions (https://*/*) Only requested optionally at runtime if the user explicitly turns on the experimental inline-chip overlay.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not share, transmit, disclose, or sell any user information to any third parties under any circumstances.

5. How to Delete Your Data

You have complete control over your data. You can delete individual items or profiles at any time in the Options page. Uninstalling the Stashfill extension from your browser immediately and permanently erases all stored data from your device.

6. Open Source Verification

Stashfill is open source under the MIT License. Anyone can inspect, build, and verify our code at: https://github.com/stashfill/stashfill.

7. Contact

For questions or security disclosures, please email privacy@stashfill.org, visit https://stashfill.tashanto.com, or open a private advisory on GitHub.
